How to play

Memorize your bottom two cards, then hide them. On your turn draw from the deck or take the top discard and exchange it with one of your cards. A card drawn from the deck can instead be discarded to use its ability (7–8 look at yours, 9–10 look at the opponent's, 11–12 blind swap, 13 look & swap, 14 double look & swap). At any time you may match the top discard with a card of the same value from either hand — a wrong guess costs a penalty card. Lowest score wins; call KOMBIO in place of a turn to end the round.
